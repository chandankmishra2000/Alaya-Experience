About Us

We welcome you to ALAYA Experience, an unparalleled experience where luxury meets the rawness of the jungle.

Nestled in the heart of the mesmerizing wilderness of Jim Corbett, Alaya Experience stands as a sanctuary amidst the untamed beauty of nature.

The accommodation & value additions took place in the last 10 years and now Alaya Resort & Spa is a leading name in the Kyari region of the Corbett National Park.

Story About Us

Surrounded by lush forests and the melodious symphony of nature, our establishment is a haven for those seeking an escape into the natural beauty of the Corbett landscape, Alaya Experience stands as a testament to luxury, serenityand impeccable service. With a track record of excellence and a commitment to providing a world-class experience which has been recognised by Meetings Incentives Conference Events (MICE).

Mission

Alaya Experience aims to offer an unforgettable fusion of luxury and nature, where guests are embraced by the untamed beauty while experiencing impeccable hospitality.Our mission is to be a beacon of luxury, ensuring each guest discovers the essence of tranquillity, adventureand natural beauty within our sanctuary.

Vision

 We aspire to be the premier destination where nature's splendour meets refined hospitality, providing an unrivalled escape for our guests.We envision creating an ambiance where each guest not only witnesses but becomes a part of the conservation efforts, fostering a deep appreciation for the wildlife, culture, and the natural bounty of Jim Corbett.
