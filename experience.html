Experience



Sitabani Temple

The temple holds deep spiritual significance, often associated with the rich cultural and religious heritage of the region. Visitors can immerse themselves in the sacred atmosphere and take part in religious ceremonies.

Garjiya Temple

Surrounded by lush greenery and flanked by the gurgle of the river Kosi, Garzia Devi temple is a beautiful centre of spirituality near Ramnagar, this is one the most important temples devoted to Goddess Parvati. The temple is built over a small hill amidst Kosi River in the beautiful environment of peace elegance.

Jungle Safari

An adventurous and educational experience that allows visitors to explore natural habitats, observe wildlife in their natural environmentand appreciate the biodiversity of a jungle or wildlife reserve.

Day Trip to Nainital

Nainital, nestled in the Kumaon foothills of the Himalayas, is a gem that beckons travellers with its pristine lakes, lush landscapes, and charming hill station allure. A day trip to Nainital promises a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural richness, and moments of sheer tranquillity.
